# Managing Volunteer Tasks

Volunteer tasks, events and approvals are managed in the member portal. The old spreadsheet is no longer used. Do not add anything to it.

Admins, board members and Resource Managers open **Volunteer** from the portal menu to create tasks, run events and review members' completed work. Detailed step-by-step instructions for these screens are being written.

Members' steps are in [Volunteering at the Makerspace](/volunteer-credit).
