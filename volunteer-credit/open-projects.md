# Bounties

Bounties are tasks the makerspace wants done. The current list is displayed on the TVs around the space and in the member portal under **Available Volunteer Opportunities** on your **Home** page. To take one, click **Claim Task** there. See [Volunteering at the Makerspace](/volunteer-credit) for the steps.

## The kinds of jobs that come up

- Repairs and improvements to the space, such as lighting, wiring, painting and drywall
- Organizing and labeling, such as tool storage, fasteners and consumables
- Shop upkeep, such as sharpening and organizing drill bits, repairing hand tools and cleaning machines
- Hauling scrap metal to the scrap yard
- Building and maintaining signs and displays
- Cleaning the laser cutter lens and mirrors. Only do this if you know what you are doing.
