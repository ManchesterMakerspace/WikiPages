# Welcome to Manchester Makerspace

Manchester Makerspace is a member-run workshop. Members have 24/7 access with their key fob, and the makerspace is run entirely by volunteers. This wiki explains how things work: how to join, how to use the shops and tools safely, and how to use the member portal.

## New to the Makerspace?

> **Start here:**
> 1. [Sign up](/digital-makerspace/sign-up) in the member portal and sign the agreements.
> 2. Come to an open house for your [orientation](/our-facilities/orientation) and your key fob. Open house is on Mondays from 6 to 8pm. See [General Information](/info) for details.
> 3. Accept your Slack invitation (check your email). Slack is where members talk to each other and to the board.
> 4. Sign in to the member portal and see [your Home page and menu](/digital-makerspace/members/home-and-navigation).
> 5. Ask for the tool checkouts you need: [Request a Tool Checkout](/digital-makerspace/members/request-a-checkout).
{.is-info}

## Need help?

- **Ask the board:** post in #discuss-talk-to-the-board on Slack, or stop in at an open house. See [FAQ](/FAQ).
- **A shop question:** post in that shop's Slack channel and @mention the Resource Manager, rather than sending a direct message. Volunteers have day jobs, so a public question often gets the fastest answer.
- **General information:** hours, board meetings and storage are in [General Information](/info).
- **Is Slack down?** [Slack status](https://slack-status.com/). **Forgot your Slack password?** [Reset it here](https://slack.com/help/articles/201909068-Reset-your-password).
