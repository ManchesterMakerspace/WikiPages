# Frequently Asked Questions

Questions that are asked often are also answered in the **#discuss-talk-to-the-board** channel on Slack.

## Joining

**How do I join?**
Sign up in the member portal and sign the agreements. See [Sign Up](/digital-makerspace/sign-up).

**How do I get my key fob?**
Come to an open house for your orientation and your fob, or ask in #discuss-talk-to-the-board. Your membership time starts when you receive your fob. See [New Member Orientation](/our-facilities/orientation).

**When can I use the space?**
Members have 24/7 access with their key fob. Open house is on Mondays from 6 to 8pm. See [General Information](/info).

## Money

**How do I pay my dues?**
See [Making Payments](/digital-makerspace/payments). A subscription renews automatically.

**How do I stop my membership?**
Cancel your subscription before the renewal date. See [Manage Membership](/digital-makerspace/manage-membership).

**Can I get a refund?**
Refunds are generally not given. See [Payment History and Refunds](/digital-makerspace/refunds).

## Using the shops

**How do I get checked out on a tool?**
Request it in the member portal, or scan the Checkout Required sign on the tool. See [Request a Tool Checkout](/digital-makerspace/members/request-a-checkout).

**Which shops are there?**
See [Shops in Makerspace](/workshops).

**Who do I ask about a shop?**
Post in that shop's Slack channel and @mention the Resource Manager. Public questions usually get faster answers than direct messages.

## Storage and parking

**Can I leave my project here?**
Only in a storage space you rent. Otherwise the policy is carry-in, carry-out. See [Storage Rentals](/rentals) and [Rent a Storage Space](/digital-makerspace/members/rent-a-space).

**Where do I park?**
See [Our Facilities](/our-facilities).

## Helping out

**How do I volunteer?**
See [Volunteering at the Makerspace](/volunteer-credit).

## Something is not working

See [Troubleshooting](/digital-makerspace/troubleshooting), or ask in #discuss-talk-to-the-board.
