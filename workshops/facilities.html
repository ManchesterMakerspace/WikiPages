Facilities and Operations

Facilities and Operations is not a shop. It covers the general areas that do not belong to a specific shop, such as the lobby and the classroom, and all aspects of daily operations not handled by a specific shop or Resource Manager. Contact the board with questions.

General Supplies

Cleaning supplies, paper towels, trash bags and the like can be found in the marked cupboard and in the marked bin in the locker room

Tour-Guide

A “tour guide” is a member who is checked out to give tours and orientations. To become one, request the checkout in the member portal. See How to request a checkout.

Special Rules

  • Clean up after yourself. If you leave a mess, used fluids or parts behind, you will be subject to a cleanup fee, and may have your tool checkouts revoked. Repeated offenses may result in your membership being terminated.
