Special Rules

  • Checkout is required before using powder coat oven
  • If you need to leave something to dry, post to #shop-paint and also put a note on your item (or on the table) with your contact info and estimated date and time when the item will be safe to move.
  • Oil, paint thinner, and other contaminated or open fluids must be properly disposed of; it should not be thrown away, and it should be not left lying around.
  • Clean up after yourself. If you leave a mess, used fluids or parts behind, you will be subject to a cleanup fee, and may have your tool checkouts revoked. Repeated offenses may result in your membership being terminated.
