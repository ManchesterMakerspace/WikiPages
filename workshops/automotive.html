Special Rules

  • Checkout is required before using the bay or any of the bay tools. It is quick and free, just ask in the channel or during open house
  • Reserve use ahead of time in #shop-automotive via our bot!  Use of the auto bay is limited to a maximum of 8 hours in any 24 hour period. Anything longer must be coordinated with the resource manager or the board in advance, and there is a fee.
  • Clean up after yourself. Used oil must be disposed of properly: never throw it in the trash, pour it out, or leave it lying around. Most auto parts stores accept used oil for free, so take it there. If you fail to clean up after yourself, or leave used fluids or parts behind, you will be subject to a cleanup fee and may have your bay privileges (checkout) revoked. Repeated offenses may result in your membership being terminated.
