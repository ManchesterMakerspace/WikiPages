# Volunteering at the Makerspace

Manchester Makerspace is run by its members. Volunteering is how we keep the shops clean, the tools working and the space improving, and it is a good way to meet other members.

There are three ways to take part.

## Bounties

Bounties are tasks the makerspace wants done. They are displayed on the TVs around the space and in the member portal. A bounty can be a repair, an improvement project or a chore that needs doing.

1. Sign in to the member portal. Open tasks appear on your **Home** page under **Available Volunteer Opportunities**.
2. Click **Claim Task** on the one you want.
3. Do the work. Your claimed tasks are listed in the **Volunteer** tab on your profile, under **My Active Claims**.
4. When you finish, select the task there and choose **Mark Complete**.
5. A volunteer approver reviews it. Approvers are volunteers too, so please be patient. Your volunteer activity is reflected in your totals the month after you do the work.

Some tasks can only be claimed once, some can be repeated, and some come back on a schedule. The task description tells you which.

See [Bounties](/volunteer-credit/open-projects) for the kinds of jobs that come up.

## Monthly volunteer and cleanup days

Once a month the makerspace holds a volunteer and cleanup day. Everyone is welcome. Watch Slack and the calendar for the next one. Events also appear on your Home page under **Available Volunteer Opportunities** with a **Join Event** button, so sign up there. Event activity is recorded after the organizers close the event.

## Ongoing jobs

Some jobs are always needed, such as sweeping, wiping down surfaces and tidying a shop. If you see something that needs doing, do it. If a job is listed as a bounty, claim it first so your work is recorded.

## Questions

Ask in **#discuss-talk-to-the-board** on Slack.
