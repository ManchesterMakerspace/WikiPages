# Payment History and Refunds

## Refunds

Payments are generally not refunded. To avoid being charged for a membership period you do not want, **stop your subscription before the renewal date**. See [Manage Membership](/digital-makerspace/manage-membership).

If you believe you are owed a refund, email **contact@manchestermakerspace.org** and explain what happened. If your question is about something else, ask in **#discuss-talk-to-the-board** on Slack.

## Review your payment history

1. Sign in to the member portal and open **My Profile** from the menu.
2. Open the **Payment History** tab to see what you have paid, with dates and amounts.

Pending payments may take a short while to show as paid. For more on paying, see [Making Payments](/digital-makerspace/payments).
