# Making Payments

Subscription payments happen automatically once you have set up your subscription. You only pay by hand for one-time charges, such as an invoice, a rental or a shop fee, and for the first payment on a new membership.

## Find what you owe

- **Home page:** **Open unpaid invoices** lists everything currently due, with **Pay** and **View** buttons. A subscription shows **Manage Subscription** instead of **Pay**, because it is paid automatically. If it says **No unpaid invoices are currently due**, you are up to date.
- **Your profile:** open **My Profile** and the **Dues** tab. It lists each due with its type, due date, amount and status.

## Pay an invoice

1. In the **Dues** tab, tick the dues you want to pay and choose **Pay Selected Dues**. On your Home page, choose **Pay** beside an invoice.
2. **Step 1, Select a payment method.** Pick a saved payment method, or choose **Add New Payment Method** to add a card, PayPal or Venmo. Choose **Next**. If you pay for a subscription, the method you use becomes your default for later payments unless you change it.
3. **Step 2, Review and confirm purchase.** Check the items and the total, then choose **Submit Payment**. Choose **Back** to change something.
4. You see **Payment Successful!** and a link to **View Receipt**. Keep the receipt for your records.

It can take a moment to finish. Wait for the confirmation before you leave the page.

## Payment methods

You can add, change and remove payment methods in your account settings. See [Manage Membership](/digital-makerspace/manage-membership#add-or-remove-a-payment-method).

## Review what you have paid

Open **My Profile** and the **Payment History** tab. It lists the date, amount, description and status of each payment, with a **View Transaction** button for details, **Filters**, and **Download Table**. See [Payment History and Refunds](/digital-makerspace/refunds).

## If a payment fails

You get an email, and a Slack message if your Slack account is linked, with the reason. Update your payment method and pay again. See [Troubleshooting](/digital-makerspace/troubleshooting).
