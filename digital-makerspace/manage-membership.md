# Manage Membership

You manage your membership in your account settings. Sign in to the member portal at **members.manchestermakerspace.org**, then open **Account Settings** from your Home page or your profile. You can also choose **Subscriptions** or **Payment Methods** straight from the portal menu.

The settings page has five sections down the left side:

| Section | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Personal Information** | Your name, email, phone and address. Also a choice to receive emails (this excludes membership notifications). |
| **Subscriptions** | Your membership: its status, type and next payment, with buttons to change the payment method or cancel. |
| **Payment Methods** | Add or remove cards, PayPal and Venmo. |
| **Transactions** | Your payment history. |
| **Security** | Change your password and turn on two-factor authentication. |

## See your membership

Open **Subscriptions**. It shows your membership **Status**, **Type** and **Next Payment** date. Your **Home** page and profile show your membership type, status and **Membership Expiration** date.

![Subscriptions](/images/membership/subscriptions.png){width=700px}

## Start or restart a membership

If you do not have a subscription, the **Subscriptions** section shows your membership details and a button, **Create Membership** (or **Update Membership** if you have had one before). Choose it and follow the steps to choose a membership and pay. They are the same steps as in [Sign Up](/digital-makerspace/sign-up). When you finish you return to your profile.

If your membership type says **Managed by PayPal**, you must first cancel the subscription in PayPal. See the end of this page.

## Pause a membership

Memberships cannot be paused. To put your membership on hold and stop the charges, cancel your subscription before the next renewal date, as below.

**Save your key fob.** When you start a membership again, your old key fob works again straight away. If you need a new fob, ask in **#discuss-talk-to-the-board** on Slack or come to an open house.

## Cancel your subscription

Do this before your renewal date, because payments are generally not refunded. See [Payment History and Refunds](/digital-makerspace/refunds).

1. Open **Subscriptions** in your settings.
2. Choose **Cancel Subscription**.
3. Read the message and choose **Submit**. Choose **Close** to keep your subscription.

Your key fob stays active until your expiration date. You can see the date on your profile. Cancelling cannot be undone, so to come back you start a new membership.

![Cancel Subscription](/images/membership/cancel-subscription.png){width=700px}

## Change your membership type

Cancel your current membership as above, then start the new one. When the new membership starts, your payment method is charged straight away and the new period is added to your existing expiration date, so you keep the time left on the old one.

## Change the payment method for your subscription

1. Open **Subscriptions** in your settings and choose **Change Payment Method**.
2. Pick one of your saved payment methods, or choose **Add New Payment Method**.
3. Choose **Submit**.

The method you pick is used for the next payment.

![Change Payment Method](/images/membership/change-payment-method.png){width=700px}

## Add or remove a payment method

Open **Payment Methods** in your settings.

- To add one, choose **Add New Payment Method**, then pick **Debit or Credit Card**, **PayPal** or **Venmo**. For a card, fill in the card number, expiration date, security code, cardholder name and postal code, and choose **Save Card**. For PayPal or Venmo, follow the prompts.
- To remove one, choose **Delete Payment Method** and confirm.

![Payment Methods](/images/membership/payment-methods.png){width=700px}

## If your membership is managed by PayPal

Some older memberships are paid through a PayPal subscription. The portal cannot change those. To change or end one, first cancel it in PayPal:

1. Sign in to paypal.com and open **Settings** (the gear icon), then the **Payments** tab. You can go straight to https://www.paypal.com/myaccount/settings/payments.
2. Select the Manchester Makerspace membership, choose **Cancel**, and confirm.
3. Then follow the steps above for the change you want.
