# Join the Makerspace

You sign up in the member portal at **members.manchestermakerspace.org**. It takes a few minutes. You create your account, sign the agreements, choose a membership, and pay. Then you come to an open house for your orientation and your key fob.

If an admin has already created an account for you, you do not need to sign up. Use **Forgot your password?** on the [Log In](/digital-makerspace/log-in) page to set a password.

## Start

On the front page choose **Sign Up**, or the **Sign Up** button beside a membership option. You can also start from a link you were given, such as a partner discount link. Those links pick the right membership and discount for you.

The sign-up form has five steps along the top: **Basic Info**, **Agreements**, **Membership**, **Payment** and **Review**.

## Step 1: Basic Info

Fill in the **Create an Account** form: your name, mailing address, an optional phone number, your email address (twice) and a password. We ask for your mailing address because it is verified when you receive your key fob. We do not sell your personal information.

The form also asks **How did you hear about us?** Please choose the closest answer. If you took a class, a box asks which one. If a friend or member told you, a box asks who. It does not affect your membership. It helps us see which classes and referrals bring people in.

Choose **Next**.

![Basic Info](/images/signup/step1-basic-info.png){width=700px}

## Step 2: Agreements

Read the **Code of Conduct** and the **Member Contract**. Tick the box for each to say you have read and agree, then sign with your mouse or your finger in the signature box. Use **Reset Signature** to start over, then choose **Next**.

![Agreements](/images/signup/step2-agreements.png){width=700px}

## Step 3: Membership

Choose the membership you want. The portal shows the options and their prices. Choose **Sign Up** beside the one you want. If you have a discount code, enter it under **Qualify for a discount?**. Choose **Next**.

- The **Household** option covers two adults in the same household.
- Some memberships are a subscription that renews automatically. Others are a single payment for a period.
- Choose **Other** (*Prepaid with class or voucher*) if you took a class or have a voucher. Choose **Continue**. Your sign-up ends there, with no payment step. Come to an open house with a photo ID to finish joining and get your key fob. You can pick a membership at any time in your account settings.

## Step 4: Payment

Pick a saved payment method, or add a debit or credit card, PayPal or Venmo. The payment method you use for a subscription becomes your default for later payments until you change it. The screen explains how recurring payments work. Read it, then choose **Next**.

If you chose **Other** in step 3, there is no payment or review step. Choosing **Continue** ends the sign-up and takes you to the portal Home page.

## Step 5: Review

Check everything. You can go back to change your membership or payment method. Then choose **Agree** if asked, and **Submit Payment** to pay. You see **Payment Successful!** and a link to **View Receipt**.

## After you sign up

You arrive on the portal's [Home page](/digital-makerspace/members/home-and-navigation) with a **Welcome!** message. Then:

1. **Accept your Slack invitation** (check your email) and your Google Drive invitation, once they arrive.
2. **Come to an open house** to complete your [orientation](/our-facilities/orientation) and receive your key fob. Open house is on Mondays from 6 to 8pm. You can also ask in **#discuss-talk-to-the-board** on Slack.
3. **Request tool checkouts** for the tools you want to use. See [Request a Tool Checkout](/digital-makerspace/members/request-a-checkout).

Your membership starts when you receive your key fob, not when you pay, so you do not lose any membership time while you wait. Any payments you made before then are applied to it. If you joined through a class, your membership starts when the class is held or when your key fob is issued.

### Reminder emails

If you sign up and do not pay or pick up a key fob, the portal emails you a reminder about a week after you sign up and another two weeks after that, and sends a Slack message if you are on Slack. If you have paid but not yet picked up your fob, you get the same kind of reminder starting about a week after your first payment. The reminders stop as soon as you have a fob, and nothing is sent more than 30 days after you sign up or pay. Bring a photo ID to the open house.

![The Welcome message](/images/signup/welcome.png){width=700px}

### Why your payment date and expiration date can differ

Your payment date is set the day you pay. Your membership term starts the day your key fob is registered, which can be a few days later. Each payment adds a billing period, usually one month, to the end of your term, so your payment will always come a few days before your membership expires. You are not paying extra and you do not lose any time. It just means you are paid up a little ahead.

For example, if you pay on the 1st and your fob is registered on the 11th, your membership runs to the 11th of the next month, and your next payment is taken on the 1st of that month, ten days before it ends. That gap stays the same for as long as you keep your subscription. Each payment still adds a full month to the end of your term.
