Subscriptions

If the portal has any problem, you can log into Braintree and do the same task there.

View and cancel subscriptions

Subscriptions cannot be paused. They can only be cancelled, and a cancelled subscription cannot be recovered.

  1. Sign in as an admin or board member, choose Billing from the portal menu, and open the Subscriptions tab.
  2. The table lists each subscription with its member, type, amount, status and next billing date. It can take a while to load.
  3. Use Filters to narrow it by the subscription's end date, its status (Active, Canceled, Past Due, Pending or Expired), and its billing plan (membership plans or rental plans).
  4. To cancel one, tick its row, click Cancel Subscription, and confirm.

A member's own subscription also appears on their profile. The Manage Subscription and View in Braintree links there open the portal's subscription screen and the subscription in Braintree. Members can cancel their own subscription or change its payment method in their account settings. See Manage Membership.

Failed subscription payments

Braintree tries failed subscription payments again automatically. When this page was written, it resubmitted after 2 days and then retried every 5 days until the payment worked or the subscription was cancelled. Check Braintree if you need to see the current schedule.

Every subscription payment, successful or not, is announced in the members relations Slack channel (set in Portal Settings; the default is members_relations). The member also gets a private Slack message. For a failure, the message includes the reason. If you are unsure what a status means, see the Braintree transaction status definitions.

Retry a subscription payment by hand

  1. Log into Braintree Gateway with the makerspace's Braintree login.
  2. Open Subscriptions from the header.
  3. Search for the subscription. To see everything that can be retried, set the Status filter to “Past Due”.
  4. In the results, click the subscription ID to see its transactions.
  5. Click Retry Charge at the top left to resubmit the payment.

Change a payment method

When a member changes their subscription's payment method, the next payment attempt uses the new one. See Manage Membership.
