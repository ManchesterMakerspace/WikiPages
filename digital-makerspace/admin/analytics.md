# Analytics

Analytics shows how the makerspace is doing: members joining and leaving, volunteer activity, and how many people use the space. Open **Analytics** from the portal menu. Only admins and board members see it. The page only shows information. Nothing you do here changes any record.

## The numbers at the top

Across the top are six counts: **Active Members**, **New This Month**, **Lost This Month**, **Subscribed**, **Past Due** and **Refunds Pending**. The page describes them as covering a rolling 30 day window. Below them are three tabs.

## Membership

- Choose a **Year**, or **All time**.
- **New vs. Lost Members per Month** compares new members with lost ones. A member counts as lost only after their membership has actually expired without renewing, never for an expiration date that is still in the future.
- **Activated Member Count Over Time** shows members who have an unexpired membership and an activated fob, month by month. It is built from a daily snapshot, so a range from before the snapshots began can be empty.

## Volunteer

Choose a **Year** or **All time**. You see how many volunteer entries and how many points were issued, and how many are still **Pending**, then charts of points issued per month, tasks completed per month, and **Top Volunteers**. The chart labels still say "credits". Read it as volunteer points. Top Volunteers shows member names, so share screenshots carefully.

## Space Usage

Counts how many different members came through the door, from door access card scans. A member counts once per day however many times they come in. Choose **Monthly**, **Daily** or **Rolling 30 Days**, and for monthly and daily views pick the year and month. The chart notes the peak number of members in a single day or month, and says how far back the data goes.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A chart is empty | There may be no data for that range. For the member count, the daily snapshot must have run. Try **All time**. |
| Numbers differ from the member list | Analytics counts lost members only after expiry, and counts fobs that are activated. |
| You cannot see Analytics | It is only for admins and board members. |
