# Shop Fees

A shop fee is a one-off charge to a member, for example for a replacement tool, parts or materials. You build it as an invoice. The member is emailed and pays it from the **Invoices** tab of their own profile, using their card on file.

Admins and board members open **Shop Fees** from the portal menu. Resource Managers can start a charge from a member's profile with the **Shop Fee** button. You cannot charge yourself, so the button is not on your own profile.

The page has two tabs: **Send Charge** and **Fee Catalog**.

## Send a charge

1. Open **Shop Fees**. Or open the member's profile and choose **Shop Fee**, which fills in the member for you.
2. Under **Member**, search by name or email and pick the member.
3. Under **Line Items**, pick an item from the catalog, or type a custom **Item Name**. Add an optional **Description**, the **Unit Price ($)** and the **Qty**. The line total is shown.
4. To add more items, choose **Add Another Item**. With more than one item, an **Invoice Label** is required. It becomes the invoice's name, for example the shop the charges came from. With a single item, the item's name is used.
5. Check the **Total** and the member's name, then choose **Generate Invoice**.
6. Read the **Confirm Invoice** box and choose **Send Invoice**.

The page confirms that the invoice was sent and that the member will receive an email. Choose **Send Another** to make the next one. Each line needs a name, a price above zero and a quantity.

You can change a catalog item's price on a line without changing the catalog. If you do, a box offers **Also update catalog price**, which saves the new price as the item's price. For a custom item, **Save to the catalog for future use** adds it to the catalog.

The invoice appears on the member's profile with their other invoices. See [Billing Options](/digital-makerspace/admin/billing-options) for how invoices are shown, settled or removed.

## Fee Catalog

The **Fee Catalog** is the list of ready-made items offered on the Send Charge tab. Each item has a name, a description and a unit price, and is shown as **Active** or **Disabled**.

- **Add Item** asks for an **Item Name**, an optional **Description** and a **Unit Price ($)**.
- Tick an item to use **Edit** or **Delete**. Delete only removes it from the list. Invoices already sent are not changed.
- Prices can always be changed when you send a charge.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **Generate Invoice** is greyed out | A member must be chosen, and every line needs a name, a price and a quantity. More than one item also needs an **Invoice Label**. |
| The member did not get the email | Check the member's email on their profile. They can also see the invoice in their **Invoices** tab. |
| The member cannot pay | They need a card on file. See [Payments](/digital-makerspace/payments). |
| You charged the wrong amount | Delete the invoice from the **Outstanding Invoices** tab of Billing Central before it is paid, then send a new one. If it is paid, refund the payment. See [Transactions](/digital-makerspace/admin/transactions). |
